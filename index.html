02About

Design, built by hand

I turn ideas into polished, player-centric games and close the gap between creative vision and technical execution.

With a foundation in both design and development, I specialise in crafting engaging gameplay loops, deep progression systems and balanced game economies, across every platform I've shipped on.

My technical background in Unity and Unreal Engine means I prototype my own mechanics, tune combat systems and build 3D environments, so what ships feels the way it was designed to.
