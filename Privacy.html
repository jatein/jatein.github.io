Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

G Squad Studio ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe, secure environment for all players. This Privacy Policy outlines our data practices regarding our premium game, BREACH.

1. Data Collection and Usage

We respect your right to privacy. We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal user data.

BREACH is designed to run entirely locally on your device. Any game progress, save states, or settings are stored securely on your own hardware and are never transmitted to our servers or any external databases.

2. Advertising and Tracking

BREACH is a premium, paid game experience. To ensure the highest quality of gameplay and privacy:

  • There are absolutely no advertisements of any kind within the game.
  • There are no third-party tracking SDKs, analytics trackers, or data-gathering tools integrated into our software.

3. Children's Privacy

BREACH is a kid-friendly game built for players of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information from any of our users, we strictly comply with the highest standards of children's digital safety, including:

  • The Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
  • Google Play's Families Policy requirements.

Parents can feel completely secure allowing their children to play BREACH, knowing that no data is being monitored, collected, or shared.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if our practices or legal obligations change. However, our commitment to maintaining a tracker-free, premium experience will remain constant. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
