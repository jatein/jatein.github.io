Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

G Squad Studio ("we", "us") makes the mobile game BREACH. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

1. Information We Collect

  • Gameplay progress. The levels you unlocked, your current level, tutorial progress, and your hint count. It is stored on your device. If you sign in with Google Play Games, it is also stored in your Google Play Games saved game so it can follow you to other devices.
  • Google Play Games information. If you sign in, Google provides your player ID and display name to the game. We use them only to sign you in and to load and save your progress. We do not receive your email address or your password.
  • Analytics and crash data. We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to learn how the game is played and to fix crashes. They collect things like which levels you start, complete or skip, whether you used a hint, device model, operating system version, app version, approximate region, an app-instance ID, and crash logs.
  • Advertising data. The game shows rewarded video ads through Unity LevelPlay and its ad network partners. These partners may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads.
  • Device settings. Sound, music and vibration preferences are stored on your device only.

2. How We Use Information

To run the game, save and restore your progress, understand and improve gameplay, fix bugs and crashes, show ads, and keep the game secure.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers above (Google/Firebase, Google Play Games, Unity LevelPlay and its advertising partners), who process it under their own privacy policies:

  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity LevelPlay: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

4. Your Choices

  • Google Play Games: you can sign in or sign out at any time in the game's Settings. Signing out stops the game from using your Google Play Games account. You can delete your saved game in the Play Games app.
  • Ads personalization: you can reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the game removes the local data. To delete cloud-saved or analytics data, contact us at jatinnakashe@gmail.com.

5. Children

BREACH is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us at jatinnakashe@gmail.com.

6. Data Retention

Local data stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Cloud saves stay in your Google account until deleted. Analytics data is kept according to Firebase's retention settings.

7. Security

We rely on Google's and our providers' security measures. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use after a change means you accept it.
