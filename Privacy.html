This policy explains what information G Squad Studio ("we", "us") collects when you play our games, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It covers every game we publish, on mobile, web or any other platform, unless a game shows its own policy.

01At a glance

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Your progress and settings are mostly stored on your own device.
  • We use trusted providers for sign-in, cloud saves, analytics, crash reports and ads, and they only receive what they need to do that job.
  • You can sign out, limit ad personalisation, or ask us to delete your data at any time.

02Information we collect

What a game collects depends on its features. Across our games, it can include:

  • Gameplay data. Levels unlocked, current progress, scores, achievements, hints or in-game currency, and tutorial progress. This is stored on your device, and in the cloud if you use a platform save feature.
  • Settings. Sound, music, vibration and similar preferences, stored on your device only.
  • Platform account information. If you sign in with a platform account such as Google Play Games, the platform shares a player ID and display name with the game so we can sign you in and save your progress. We never receive your password, and we do not receive your email address through sign-in.
  • Analytics and crash data. Through services such as Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics: which levels you start, finish or skip, feature usage, device model, operating system and app version, approximate region, an app-instance identifier, and crash logs.
  • Advertising data. In games that show ads, our ad mediation provider (such as Unity LevelPlay) and its partners may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and how you interact with ads, to deliver ads, limit repeats, measure performance and prevent fraud.
  • Purchases. If a game offers in-app purchases, payment is handled entirely by the store (for example Google Play). We receive confirmation of what you bought, never your card or payment details.
  • Information you send us. If you email us for support or feedback, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include.

03How we use it

  • To run the game, sign you in, and save and restore your progress.
  • To understand how the game is played, balance difficulty and improve future updates.
  • To find and fix bugs and crashes.
  • To show ads that keep our games free, and to measure them.
  • To deliver purchases and handle refunds or support requests.
  • To keep our games secure and prevent cheating, fraud and abuse.
  • To meet legal obligations.

04Legal bases

Where laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on: contract (to provide the game you chose to play), legitimate interests (to improve, secure and support our games), consent (for personalised advertising and any other processing where the law requires it), and legal obligation. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing that already took place.

05Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for money. We share data only with:

  • Service providers that help us run our games, such as Google (Firebase, Google Play Games), Unity LevelPlay and its advertising partners, and the web platforms that host our browser games. They process data under their own privacy policies.
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect the rights, property or safety of our players, us or others.
  • A successor, if our studio or a game is sold or merged, under the same protections described here.

Key provider policies: Google policies.google.com/privacy and Unity unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

06Your choices and rights

  • Sign-in: signing in is optional. You can sign in or out from a game's settings, and delete platform saved games from the platform's app.
  • Ad personalisation: on Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. On iOS, use the App Tracking Transparency prompt or your privacy settings. Where required, our games ask for consent before showing personalised ads.
  • Local data: uninstalling a game, or clearing your browser data for web games, removes data stored on your device.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, California law or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, or receive a copy of your data. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email gsquadstudio@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days and may need to confirm the request is yours.

07Children

Our games are made for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact gsquadstudio@gmail.com and we will delete it.

08Data retention

Data on your device stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Cloud saves stay in your platform account until you delete them. Analytics and crash data are kept only as long as needed for the purposes above, under each provider's retention settings. Support emails are kept while we resolve your request and for a reasonable time afterwards.

09International transfers

We are based in India, and our providers may process data in other countries. Where data leaves your region, it is protected by the providers' safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses.

10Security

We rely on the security measures of the platforms and providers we use, and we limit the data we collect. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our games or the law change. The date at the top shows the latest version. For significant changes we will give notice in our games or on this page. Continuing to play after an update means you accept it.
