02Pitches

What if…

  1. 01 10 Minutes Ahead Stealth strategy ↗

    A stealth-strategy game where you experience events 10 minutes before they occur, giving you the chance to prevent or manipulate them.
  2. 02 Reverse Heist Stealth action ↗

    A stealth-action game that inverts the traditional heist: infiltrate highly secured locations to quietly return dangerous, cursed artifacts.
  3. 03 Shadow Match 3D puzzle ↗

    A relaxing 3D puzzle game where players rotate and align abstract objects until they perfectly match a target shadow silhouette.
  4. 04 Power Debt Action roguelite ↗

    An action roguelite where every overpowered hack-and-slash ability is free upfront, but creates a chaotic debt the world will unpredictably collect later.
  5. 05 Museum of Lives Narrative adventure ↗

    A narrative adventure where you wake inside a museum of unfinished lives, reconstructing broken memories before realising you might be an exhibit yourself.
  6. 06 Quiet Sea Narrative mystery ↗

    A deep-sea mystery where an isolated communication engineer repairs an abandoned station that is secretly receiving signals from the future.
  7. 07 Different Physics Party platformer ↗

    A chaotic party platformer where everyone plays the same game, but each player follows a completely different set of randomised physics rules.
  8. 08 Background Character Choice-driven narrative ↗

    You are an ordinary NPC. Your small, mundane actions quietly create butterfly effects that change how the hero's grand quest ends.
  9. 09 Time Bankruptcy Action roguelite ↗

    An action roguelite with devastating temporal abilities, where every attack or dodge ages you and burns through your own limited lifespan.
  10. 10 Sin Hunter Hack and slash ↗

    Hunt the living embodiments of the Seven Deadly Sins, then choose to purify their corrupting power or wield it yourself.
  11. 11 The Man Who Can Pause the World Supernatural thriller ↗

    You can freeze time, but the temporal entity that gave you the power is secretly learning how to enter reality through you.
  12. 12 The Reverse War Sci-fi thriller ↗

    Fight a hidden war across forward and reversed timelines, coordinating actions from opposite directions in time.
  13. 13 The Dream Invasion Action adventure ↗

    Humanity's worst nightmares manifest in the real world, and you fight back with unstable supernatural powers.
